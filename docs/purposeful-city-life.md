# Place-aware resident activities

Resident schedules use short, purposeful routes: work, market, quay, patrol, gathering and visitor destinations. Resident identity, city map, jobs and save format are authoritative; rendering quality does not modify them.

Activity goals must pass terrain and navigation checks. Unsafe or blocked goals are skipped or retried through bounded local work. Nearby active simulation is capped for predictable CPU cost while the complete population remains available in the world.

See [living city](city-life.md) and [world planning](world-plan.md).
