# Building variety and architectural identity

The ten districts of Jade Harbor use different architectural families while sharing a coherent Chinatown-inspired visual language. Generator 10 expands the range of physical building compositions inside the established city footprint.

## What makes buildings different

Variation is visible in the building itself, not only in its color or name.

| Design element | Examples |
|---|---|
| Overall shape | Main hall, rear rise, corner tower, split-height building, terraced body, side wing, gate house, and pavilion wing |
| Roof | Gable, hip, clipped, layered, offset, cross, pavilion, split ridge, terrace, and quay eave |
| Facade | Lattice, ribbon windows, shutters, timber grids, stone bases, arcades, screens, and recessed bays |
| Street frontage | Flush shopfront, canopy, arcade, recessed porch, framed gate, and double shop |
| Composition | Different heights, roof directions, bay rhythms, courtyards, and relationships between front and rear sections |

District identity guides which combinations appear. A market street, a quiet hutong, a harbor block, and a scholar garden should not share one repeated silhouette.

## Variety without losing density

Buildings remain within the established parcels and occupied ground areas. The city does not become more varied by expanding the map, leaving lots empty, widening setbacks, or removing existing structures.

Roof forms, balconies, signs, and facade details are designed to connect to supporting walls, beams, decks, or foundations. This keeps visual variation tied to coherent building shapes.

## How variety is assessed

The design system is evaluated through emitted building geometry, district coverage, neighboring repetition, retained parcel occupancy, and visual review. A controlled design witness produces more than 25 distinct complete voxel outputs from combinations of roof direction, facade, and frontage choices. This is evidence of a larger design space, not a claim that every building is unique or that human perception has an exact numerical multiplier.
