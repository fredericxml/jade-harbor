# Urban density and access

Jade Harbor's procedural metropolis prioritizes an occupied, walkable Chinatown rather than isolated decorative buildings. District infill preserves important streets, courtyards, public access, entry links, road grades and landmark reservations. Foundations adapt to local terrain without floating structures.

Generic forest is intentionally limited around the authored city and travel network. The world retains meaningful rural and landscape scenery outside the city while preserving landmark approaches and navigation links. NPC and player collision use the authoritative generated blocks; distant rendering does not change these rules.

Worlds use the single built-in terrain layout. Saves containing obsolete generator selectors are rejected. See [world planning](world-plan.md), [architecture](architecture.md) and [feature matrix](feature-matrix.md).
