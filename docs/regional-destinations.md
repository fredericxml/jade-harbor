# Regional destination program

Generator 9 reuses the existing macro envelope. The city, Panda court, original eight landmark reservations, river/harbor, mine and realized approaches take priority over new regional offers. Each new destination owns a stable reservation, deterministic local candidate search, terrain probes, public loop, entry and a connection to an actual road or heritage trail. It is planned before chunks are generated and indexed for independent chunk reconstruction.

| Destination | Regional context and content |
|---|---|
| Willow Valley Tulou | Southwestern communal earthen house, open court, ancestral hall, pottery house and inn |
| Amber Gate Village | Western heritage merchant lanes, enclosing wall and gate, varied low-rise houses and public street |
| Li River Stone Walk | Eastern limestone pillar landscape, contour walk, lookout tea house, trail lodge and guard post |
| Mist Terrace Tea Village | Northern hillside guild, stair-side inn, tea house, lookout and limited tea terraces |
| Lotus Causeway Gardens | Southern planted lake precinct, causeways, pavilions, tea house and garden inn |

These complement the released palace, ridge wall, excavations, guild beacon, mountain sanctuary, cliff chambers, Reed Moon Garden and western pagoda. They use those existing destinations as geographic context rather than duplicating them. Building foundations and graded public ribbons are local interventions; the macro river, ridges and world extent are retained.

The generic woodland mask is a strict subset of v8's mask. Every complete 400 × 400 supertile has exactly four potential 40 × 40 woodland cells, for a global 4% potential bound. Eligibility only removes woodland. Trunks and complete canopies stay inside that mask. Acceptance measurements sample the entire existing macro envelope at eight-block spacing and separately report urban land, water, actual generic woodland, destination precincts, village tea/orchard planting, river edge and rocky/open scenery. Managed destination planting is visibly sparse and attached to precincts; it is not a renamed random tree carpet.

The former broad cultivation matrix is removed from new worlds. Tea cultivation is confined to the northern village context; orchards belong to the southwestern village. Historic small paddy content remains. Open river edges and mountain terrain continue to frame destinations, while the finite view distance limits how much of a large landscape can be seen at once.

Each region adds sixteen permanent host/visitor identities with local work, social, patrol and visiting circuits. The 4,000 v8 identities remain; the new registry totals 4,080, within the unchanged 4,096 entity save limit. Detailed activity remains capped at 96 and navigation at 96 expanded nodes per tick. Far residents retain the existing logical schedule policy.

Existing worlds are never silently reinterpreted. **Create updated city copy** creates a separate generator 9 slot from a generator 6, 7 or 8 world, retains edits, inventory, health, discoveries and resident state, and starts at Panda Plaza. Existing residents are placed at their new routine anchors once on that explicit layout copy. Initialized generator 9 reloads retain saved positions and do not resurrect removed residents. Overlay edits remain authoritative even when they conflict with a new feature.

## Wider former-woodland conversion

The regional program also covers the complete removed v8 woodland quota inside the original envelope. Each former woodland cell is assigned to the nearest existing heritage or new regional destination and physically realizes its context: eastern karst, mountain heritage rock forms, northern tea contours, village orchard planting or curated heritage/lake gardens. These are landscape extensions of a small number of coherent regions, rather than a new famous monument in every cell. Protected city lots, roads, original landmark geometry, the mine, paddy and wilderness shrine clearances win over this layer. Actual eligible coverage is reported separately from its potential cell catalogue.

Landform columns contact natural terrain, and small curated planting replaces broad random tree carpets. The wider garden layer does not introduce unconstrained fluid sources. All placement is deterministic, uses a bounded cell index and remains independent of chunk order.
