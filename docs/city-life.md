# A living city

Jade Harbor's residents give the metropolis a sense of daily rhythm. The world contains **4,080 persistent resident identities**. Up to **96 nearby residents** can receive detailed local movement and activity at once; residents farther away keep their identity and schedule without all running full local simulation simultaneously.

Residents are connected to real places in the city. Their routines respond to profession, home and workplace, time of day, and weather.

## Meet the residents

You may encounter:

- **Merchants and shopkeepers** browsing, greeting customers, serving, or returning to their storefronts.
- **Artisans and workshop workers** inspecting materials, working at benches, carrying supplies, or maintaining displays.
- **Harbor workers** moving goods around quays, warehouses, and loading areas.
- **Guards** holding posts and patrolling supported routes, including during the night.
- **Scholars and researchers** visiting gardens, pavilions, excavations, and other public places.
- **Residents and visitors** shopping, greeting one another, meeting in public spaces, or visiting local landmarks.

Not every resident offers a trade. Right-click a nearby resident to view their name, role, and any available offers.

## Daily rhythms

The city changes as the day progresses:

| Time of day | Typical activity |
|---|---|
| Early morning | Residents begin their day; harbor work can start early. |
| Morning | Shops, workshops, public routes, and workplaces become active. |
| Midday | Shopping, short breaks, garden visits, and public activity become more visible. |
| Afternoon | Work and local errands continue. |
| Evening | Some residents gather or return home while commercial and nightlife areas remain active. |
| Night | Many residents rest; guards keep watch and selected late-opening roles remain active. |

These are broad patterns rather than a rigid timetable. Individual residents have stable variations, and not every person follows the same schedule.

## District character

Different parts of the city support different kinds of activity:

- **Old Market Maze** and **Lantern Commercial Core** emphasize browsing, storefronts, service, and evening commerce.
- **Hutong Courtyards** emphasize neighborhood visits, household errands, and quieter lane activity.
- **Jade Water Town** connects bridges, canal-side shops, tea service, and waterfront conversation.
- **Merchant Docks** support cargo handling, quay work, commerce, and guard posts.
- **Artisan Workyards** bring residents into workshops and craft spaces.
- **Scholar Gardens** and nearby public spaces support reading, study, maintenance, and garden visits.
- **Panda Heritage Core** and the **Imperial Quarter** connect public gatherings, visitors, attendants, and patrols.
- **Northern Terrace Edge** and the outer regions provide watch points, inns, village work, and scenic visits.

## Weather and social activity

Rain changes some outdoor routines. Optional social gatherings may move indoors or be cancelled, while essential work and guard patrols continue. This helps the city respond to the environment without creating a separate weather-driven economy.

## What the population represents

The 4,080 count refers to persistent resident identities, not 4,080 fully animated characters on screen at once. Detailed movement is focused around the player, while distant residents retain their identity and coarse schedule state. The city is designed to feel inhabited, but it is not a full economic or social simulation: residents do not run a complete supply chain, hold long conversations, or participate in a mission campaign.
